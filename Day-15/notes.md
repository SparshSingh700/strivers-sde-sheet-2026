# Day 15 Notes

## Activity Selection

Core Idea:

* Earliest finishing activity creates maximum room for future activities.

Mental Trigger:
"Finish early, leave space for more."

Pattern:

* Greedy

Time: O(n log n)

---

## Minimum Platforms

Core Idea:

* Platforms needed = overlapping trains.

Mental Trigger:
"Count trains currently at station."

Pattern:

* Greedy
* Two Pointers

Time: O(n log n)

---

## Job Sequencing Problem

Core Idea:

* Take highest profit jobs first.
* Schedule as late as possible before deadline.

Mental Trigger:
"Protect earlier slots for future jobs."

Pattern:

* Greedy
* DSU

Time: O(n log n)

---

## Fractional Knapsack

Core Idea:

* Value density matters, not absolute value.

Mental Trigger:
"Take the most valuable kilogram first."

Pattern:

* Greedy
* Sorting

Time: O(n log n)
